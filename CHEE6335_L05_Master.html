16 The Two Maximum-Entropy Problems Side by Side

Problem Constraints Maximum-entropy result
No additional physical constraint
Fixed average energy and

The conceptual difference is the information supplied to the maximization problem.

Without an additional constraint, the maximum-entropy distribution is uniform.

With a fixed average energy, the least-biased distribution consistent with that constraint is exponential.

توضیح فارسی
